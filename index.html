CaseLines Bundler

Preparing Court Online documents for CaseLines.

⚠ BETA 1 — FOR TESTING AND FEEDBACK

CaseLines Bundler is currently provided as a Beta version for testing by selected users and for the purpose of obtaining feedback and identifying errors or improvements.

Document classification, filing-date extraction, filing-time extraction, chronological ordering, CaseLines allocation and filename generation may contain errors.

Always review and verify the results before relying upon, submitting, uploading or otherwise using the generated documents for any legal, court or professional purpose.

🔒 Local browser processing

Your PDF documents are designed to remain on your computer and are processed locally in your web browser.

The documents are not uploaded by this application to a server operated by the application and are not intentionally transmitted to the developer for storage or processing.

Remember to save your generated ZIP file before closing this page. The application does not retain the generated bundle for later retrieval.
